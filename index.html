Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

PrintSplit includes a fully functional 14-day free trial. You can split models, generate pins, and test the 1-click slicer export with zero restrictions. No account or credit card required.

Which 3D printers and slicers are supported?

PrintSplit exports standard high-precision binary STL files compatible with all slicers (OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura, Anycubic). The 1-click launch button detects OrcaSlicer and Bambu Studio automatically.

Can I adjust the clearance between pins and holes?

Yes! FDM 3D printers typically need 0.15 mm to 0.25 mm clearance so pins slide in smoothly with a firm friction fit. You can freely adjust the clearance, pin diameter, and pin length in the settings sidebar.

Does PrintSplit run on Apple Silicon?

Yes. PrintSplit is compiled as a Universal macOS binary with full native support for Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.
